T.RAD North America selects Clarksville for first Tennessee location

Source URL: https://www.tradeandindustrydev.com/region/tennessee/news/tn-trad-north-america-selects-clarksville-first-35299

FACTUAL PARAPHRASE

T.RAD North America selected Clarksville for its first Tennessee location. The company plans to invest $90.2 million and create 928 jobs in Montgomery County.

The project calls for a new facility at the Clarksville-Montgomery County Corporate Business Park South, a Select Tennessee Certified Site. The additional capacity is intended to increase production and broaden T.RAD's customer base in the Southeast.

T.RAD North America is headquartered in Hopkinsville, Kentucky, and is a subsidiary of Japanese manufacturer T.RAD. The parent company was founded in Kawasaki, Japan, in 1936 as a radiator manufacturer and now operates on four continents.
