Airsys Opens Global Headquarters Campus in South Carolina

Source URL: https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/05/13/3294315/0/en/Airsys-Opens-Global-Headquarters-Campus-in-South-Carolina-to-Meet-Surging-Global-Demand-for-AI-and-Data-Center-Cooling.html

FACTUAL PARAPHRASE

Airsys officially opened a new global headquarters campus in Woodruff, South Carolina, in May 2026. The 60-acre campus serves as a global hub for the company's high-efficiency, zero-water cooling technologies for data centers, artificial intelligence infrastructure and other high-performance digital systems.

The site expands Airsys' U.S. manufacturing and engineering capabilities and houses corporate leadership functions. The company said the investment was expected to create 215 jobs in South Carolina's Upstate region.
