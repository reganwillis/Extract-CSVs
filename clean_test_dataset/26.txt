First Solar inaugurates $1.1 billion Alabama manufacturing facility

Source URL: https://www.madeinalabama.com/2024/09/first-solar-inaugurates-1-1-billion-alabama-manufacturing-facility/

FACTUAL PARAPHRASE

First Solar inaugurated a roughly $1.1 billion solar-module manufacturing facility in Lawrence County, Alabama, in September 2024.

The vertically integrated thin-film solar factory in Trinity was designed for approximately 3.5 gigawatts of annual manufacturing capacity and was expected to create more than 800 jobs. The project expanded First Solar's U.S. manufacturing footprint and domestic production of photovoltaic modules.
