Capgemini to Establish New Delivery Center in Nashville

Source URL: https://www.tn.gov/ecd/news/2021/11/17/governor-lee--commissioner-rolfe-announce-capgemini-to-establish-new-delivery-center-in-nashville.html

FACTUAL PARAPHRASE

Capgemini announced a $20.1 million investment to establish its first Tennessee delivery center at Broadwest in Nashville. The global IT consulting company committed to creating at least 500 jobs, with projected growth of as many as 1,000 positions.

The Nashville center was designed to help Capgemini serve clients, improve solution delivery and develop talent. Its work was expected to focus on enterprise application modernization, artificial intelligence, machine learning and Internet of Things solutions. Capgemini is headquartered in France and, at the time of the announcement, operated in about 50 countries.
