Ocean Craft Marine selects Horry County for U.S. manufacturing headquarters

Source URL: https://scbiz.com/ocean-craft-marine-horry-county-us-manufacturing-hq/

FACTUAL PARAPHRASE

Asis Boats USA, doing business as Ocean Craft Marine, selected Horry County, South Carolina, for its U.S. manufacturing headquarters. The company announced a $10.8 million investment expected to create 93 jobs.

The Dubai-headquartered business manufactures rigid inflatable boats, amphibious platforms, aluminum military craft and professional maritime vessels used by defense, rescue and government customers. Its new U.S. operation will be located at Bucksport Marine Park in Conway and is expected to begin operations by the end of 2026.
