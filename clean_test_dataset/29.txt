Riverstone Logistics relocating headquarters and establishing first South Carolina operation

Source URL: https://www.sccommerce.com/news/riverstone-logistics-relocating-headquarters-and-establishing-first-south-carolina-operation

FACTUAL PARAPHRASE

Riverstone Logistics announced that it would relocate its corporate headquarters from Charlotte, North Carolina, to Rock Hill, South Carolina, while establishing its first South Carolina operation.

The York County project represents $16.4 million in investment and is expected to create 159 jobs. Riverstone, founded in 2017, provides final-mile delivery, freight brokerage and customized logistics services, with customers in areas including consumer electronics, building materials and appliance installation.

The headquarters will be located at 220 West White Street in Rock Hill, with operations expected to be online in July 2026.
