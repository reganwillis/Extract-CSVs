WiJo Pouches establishes first U.S. manufacturing operation

Source URL: https://www.expansionsolutionsmagazine.com/wijo-pouches-establishes-first-us-manufacturing-operation/

FACTUAL PARAPHRASE

Sweden-headquartered WiJo Pouches North America selected Lexington County, South Carolina, for its first North American manufacturing operation. The company announced a $13.5 million investment expected to create 170 jobs.

WiJo is a contract manufacturer of next-generation oral pouches and offers prototyping, scalable manufacturing and market-compliance support. It planned to lease a 55,000-square-foot facility at 381 Burton Road in Lexington for contract production of nicotine, caffeine and functional pouches. Operations were expected to begin in March 2026.
