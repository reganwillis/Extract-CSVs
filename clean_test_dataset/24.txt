Foxconn plans $5 billion investment over five years in Indian facility

Source URL: https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/tech/hardware/taiwans-foxconn-plans-5-billion-investment-over-5-years-in-indian-facility/articleshow/48401822.cms?from=mdr

FACTUAL PARAPHRASE

Foxconn signed an agreement with the Indian state of Maharashtra in August 2015 covering a planned $5 billion investment over five years in a new electronics manufacturing facility.

Foxconn founder Terry Gou and Maharashtra Chief Minister Devendra Fadnavis announced the agreement in Mumbai. Foxconn said it was seeking local partners but did not specify at the time whether the facility would manufacture mobile phones.

The project supported India's 'Make in India' manufacturing initiative. Maharashtra officials said the facility could generate about 50,000 jobs. Foxconn also discussed broader plans at the time to develop multiple factories and data centers in India.
