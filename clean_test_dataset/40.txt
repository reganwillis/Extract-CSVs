Wenker establishing U.S. headquarters in Greenville County

Source URL: https://www.sccommerce.com/news/wenker-inc-growing-south-carolina-presence-establishing-us-headquarters-greenville-county

FACTUAL PARAPHRASE

German automotive supplier Wenker announced plans to expand its South Carolina presence and establish its U.S. headquarters in Greenville County. The project represents $3.1 million in investment and is expected to create 27 jobs.

Wenker specializes in metal and steel construction, plant engineering and fabricated products for the automotive industry. After operating at a smaller Spartanburg County site, the company planned a 131,400-square-foot manufacturing facility at 23095 E. Phillips Road in Greer. The larger site provides additional manufacturing capacity and space for the company's U.S. headquarters.
