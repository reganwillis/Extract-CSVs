MAHLE to expand manufacturing operations in Hamblen County

Source URL: https://www.tn.gov/ecd/news/2025/8/27/mahle-gmbh-to-expand-manufacturing-operations-in-hamblen-county.html

FACTUAL PARAPHRASE

German automotive supplier MAHLE announced an expansion at its Morristown, Tennessee, operation in Hamblen County. The project brings production of high-voltage electric air-conditioning compressors, or e-compressors, to the United States and creates nearly 50 jobs.

The Morristown plant has manufactured steel pistons for decades. With the new production line, the site will make products for both internal-combustion and electrified vehicles. MAHLE describes e-compressors as important to electric-vehicle thermal management, battery life, charging performance and driving range.
