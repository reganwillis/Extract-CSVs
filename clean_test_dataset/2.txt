Recticel Group Selects Tennessee for First U.S. Insulated Panels Facility

Date: February 24, 2026
Source: Tennessee Department of Economic and Community Development (TNECD)
Source URL: https://tnecd.com/news/recticel-group-selects-tennessee-for-first-u-s-insulated-panels-facility/

FACTUAL PARAPHRASE

Tennessee officials and Recticel Group announced that the Belgian insulation company selected Mt. Pleasant, Tennessee, for its first U.S. manufacturing facility dedicated to insulated panels.

Recticel plans to invest $49.6 million and create 78 jobs at the Cherry Glen Industrial Park in Mt. Pleasant. Cherry Glen is a Select Tennessee Certified Site. The facility will manufacture insulated panels for the U.S. market under the Trimo North America brand.

Recticel Group is headquartered in Belgium and supplies insulation products and related building solutions. At the time of the announcement, the company described itself as having a substantial European and U.S. presence. Its manufacturing network included facilities in multiple countries.

TNECD reported that Belgian companies had collectively created more than 840 jobs and invested nearly $245 million in Tennessee through foreign direct investment.

STATE AND LOCAL COMMENTS

Gov. Bill Lee said the project reflected Tennessee's appeal to international companies, citing the state's workforce and quality of life. He emphasized that Recticel's first U.S. insulated-panels operation would create additional economic opportunities in southern Middle Tennessee.

Deputy Gov. and TNECD Commissioner Stuart C. McWhorter highlighted the role of foreign direct investment in Tennessee's economic-development pipeline. He said international investment accounted for a significant share of projects secured during Gov. Lee's administration and expressed confidence that Recticel's Mt. Pleasant operation would strengthen the state's international business base.

Recticel Group CEO Jan Vergote described the launch of Trimo North America as part of the company's global growth strategy. He said demand for the company's insulated panels in Europe had been supported by their thermal performance, durability, and design flexibility. The Tennessee plant is intended to manufacture insulated panels for applications including warehouses, gymnasiums, schools, industrial buildings, and retail facilities. Vergote also said the company intended to participate actively in Tennessee's business community.

TVA economic-development vice president Heidi Smith welcomed Recticel to Maury County and credited cooperation among TVA, Mt. Pleasant Power System, TNECD, Maury Alliance, and the Middle Tennessee Industrial Development Association. She characterized the project as an investment that would create employment opportunities in the region.

Maury County Mayor Sheila Butt said the project demonstrated the ability of local, regional, and state economic-development partners to attract employers offering quality jobs. She referred to the company's planned operation as its North American headquarters and welcomed Recticel to the community.

Mt. Pleasant Mayor Bill White said the Cherry Glen Industrial Park and the city were becoming a center for foreign-direct-investment projects. He said Recticel would further diversify the local industrial economy.

State Sen. Joey Hensley said the investment would bring 78 jobs, new economic activity, and innovation to Maury County. He credited Tennessee's business environment and cooperation between local officials and TNECD.

State Rep. Kip Capley similarly pointed to Tennessee's workforce, policies, and economy as factors attracting businesses. He said the new Maury County operation would add 78 jobs and support the local economy.

ABOUT RECTICEL GROUP

Recticel Group's product portfolio includes insulation boards, insulated panels, and acoustic solutions.

Recticel Insulation develops polyurethane thermal insulation boards designed to improve building comfort and energy efficiency. Its offerings also include vacuum insulation panels through Turvac.

The company's Trimo business supplies mineral-wool insulated panels and modular-space solutions, primarily for non-residential applications. REX Panels & Profiles supplies PIR and mineral-wool roofing and wall systems. The Soundcoat Company supplies acoustic solutions used in technology applications.

At the end of 2025, Recticel Group employed 1,285 people and reported sales of EUR 655.1 million. Its operations extended across seven countries.

The Science Based Targets initiative had approved Recticel's near-term greenhouse-gas reduction targets for Scope 1, Scope 2, and Scope 3 emissions through 2030, using 2021 as the base year, as well as the company's net-zero targets for 2050. CDP had placed Recticel on its Climate Change A List.

Recticel is publicly listed on Euronext Brussels under the ticker RECT.

ABOUT TNECD

The Tennessee Department of Economic and Community Development is the state's principal economic-development agency. Its work includes recruiting new corporate investment, supporting the expansion of existing businesses, promoting job creation, and providing grants and other assistance intended to strengthen communities and improve Tennessee's business environment.
