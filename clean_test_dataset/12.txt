Hitachi Energy to Expand Manufacturing Operations in Crockett County

Source URL: https://www.tn.gov/ecd/news/2025/8/20/hitachi-energy-to-expand-manufacturing-operations-in-crockett-county.html

FACTUAL PARAPHRASE

Hitachi Energy announced a $97 million expansion of its operation in Alamo, Crockett County, Tennessee. The project is expected to create 100 jobs.

The expansion is intended to make the Crockett County site Hitachi Energy's largest North American facility producing transformer bushings and other critical electrical-grid components. The project responds to rising demand for electricity and energy infrastructure and strengthens the company's manufacturing capacity in West Tennessee.
