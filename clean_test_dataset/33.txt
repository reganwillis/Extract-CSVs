United Composite Materials selects Greenville County for first South Carolina operation

Source URL: https://www.sccommerce.com/news/united-composite-materials-llc-selects-greenville-county-first-south-carolina-operation

FACTUAL PARAPHRASE

United Composite Materials LLC selected Greenville County for its first South Carolina operation. The advanced carbon-composite materials manufacturer announced a $17.5 million investment expected to create 50 jobs.

The operation is located at 1446 S. Buncombe Road in Greer. The company plans to use two existing facilities at the property as specialized manufacturing plants supplying advanced carbon-composite materials to North American sports and leisure markets. Operations were expected to begin in July 2026. South Carolina approved job-development credits related to the project.
