Biederman to Establish First U.S. Location in Hawkins County

Source URL: https://www.greenevillesun.com/business/local_business_news/biederman-to-establish-first-u-s-location-in-hawkins-county/article_38291875-7998-4bdc-bf9d-4f8c9f7fc228.html

FACTUAL PARAPHRASE

Biederman announced plans to establish its first U.S. facility in Rogersville, Hawkins County, Tennessee. The Canadian manufacturer plans to invest $4.4 million and create 48 jobs.

The Hawkins County facility is to be upgraded to increase manufacturing capacity, broaden the company's product offerings and strengthen its brand presence. Founded in 1992 and based in Ontario, Canada, Biederman manufactures grease cartridges and distributes them to customers internationally.
