Reinhausen to Expand Production Facility in Gibson County

Source URL: https://tnecd.com/news/reinhausen-to-expand-production-facility-in-gibson-county/

FACTUAL PARAPHRASE

Reinhausen Inc. announced an expansion of its production facility in Humboldt, Gibson County, Tennessee. The company plans to invest $6.7 million and create 90 jobs. The expansion is intended to meet rising customer demand for transformer production, voltage-regulation equipment and grid-automation technologies.

Reinhausen traces its history to Germany in 1868. It entered the United States in 1987 with operations in Alamo, Tennessee, and later moved its U.S. hub to Humboldt. The company now operates across six continents and employs about 5,000 people globally. The announcement also emphasized Germany's importance as a source of foreign direct investment in Tennessee.
