Hi-Chew candy maker Morinaga creating jobs and building second Orange County factory

Source URL: https://www.wral.com/archive/21527447/

FACTUAL PARAPHRASE

Japanese confectionery company Morinaga & Co. announced plans for a second factory in Orange County, North Carolina, to expand production of Hi-Chew candy. The company said it would invest $136 million in a 133,000-square-foot expansion at its Mebane site and create 204 jobs.

Construction was planned to begin in October 2024, with production expected in early 2027. Morinaga established its U.S. division in 2013 and opened its first Mebane plant in 2015. The new jobs include production, quality-assurance, maintenance and support roles.

The state approved a performance-based One North Carolina Fund grant of $100,000, subject to investment, job-creation and local-support requirements.
