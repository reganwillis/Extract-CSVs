Airsys Opens Global Headquarters Campus in South Carolina

Source URL: https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/05/13/3294315/0/en/Airsys-Opens-Global-Headquarters-Campus-in-South-Carolina-to-Meet-Surging-Global-Demand-for-AI-and-Data-Center-Cooling.html

FACTUAL PARAPHRASE

Airsys officially opened a 60-acre global headquarters campus in Woodruff, South Carolina, in May 2026. The site is a hub for manufacturing, engineering and corporate leadership focused on high-efficiency, zero-water cooling technology for AI, data-center and other mission-critical digital infrastructure.
