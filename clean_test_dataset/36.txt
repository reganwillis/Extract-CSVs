Kadia USA moving U.S. headquarters to South Carolina

Source URL: https://scbiz.com/kadia-usa-headquarters-relocates-to-piedmont-sc/

FACTUAL PARAPHRASE

Kadia USA announced that it would relocate its U.S. headquarters from Michigan to Piedmont, South Carolina, near Greenville. The company specializes in precision-machining technology, particularly honing and mechanical deburring equipment, tools and abrasives.

Kadia's global headquarters are in Stuttgart, Germany. The South Carolina project followed a roughly two-year site-selection and planning process. Construction was expected to begin in July 2025, with the new headquarters scheduled for completion during the first half of 2026.

The facility is intended to serve as the hub for Kadia's U.S. operations, bringing the company closer to customers and shortening response times in the American market.
