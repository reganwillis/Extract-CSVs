Volvo Cars selects South Carolina for first American plant

Source URL: https://www.sccommerce.com/news/volvo-cars-selects-south-carolina-first-american-plant

FACTUAL PARAPHRASE

Volvo Cars selected Berkeley County, South Carolina, for its first manufacturing plant in the Western Hemisphere. The company announced an investment of up to $500 million.

The factory, located near Charleston on the Camp Hall site, was planned with initial annual capacity of about 100,000 vehicles. It would manufacture newer-generation Volvo models for both the U.S. market and export. Construction was expected to begin in fall 2015 and vehicle production in 2018.

Volvo projected approximately 2,000 jobs over the following decade and as many as 4,000 jobs over the longer term. The company cited port and infrastructure access, workforce quality, the investment environment and South Carolina's high-tech manufacturing experience as reasons for the location decision.
