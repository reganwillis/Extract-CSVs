South Carolina industry recruitment reaches $9.12 billion in 2025

Source URL: https://governor.sc.gov/news/2026-01/south-carolina-industry-recruitment-reaches-912-billion-2025

FACTUAL PARAPHRASE

South Carolina reported $9.12 billion in announced capital investment from industry recruitment during 2025, the state's third-highest annual total on record. Projects announced during the year were expected to create more than 8,100 jobs.

More than 40% of both announced capital investment and new jobs were associated with rural areas, the highest rural shares reported since 2010. State officials presented the results as evidence that economic-development activity was spreading beyond South Carolina's major metropolitan areas.
