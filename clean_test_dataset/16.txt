Toyota to invest $1 billion on new Mexico plant, create 2,400 jobs

Source URL: https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/toyota-to-invest-$1-billion-on-new-mexico-plant,-create-2,400-jobs:-source-336893

FACTUAL PARAPHRASE

Reuters reported in April 2015 that Toyota planned to spend about $1 billion on a new automobile factory in Mexico. Sources familiar with the plan said the plant would be located in the central state of Guanajuato.

The proposed factory was expected to have annual capacity of about 200,000 vehicles and create roughly 2,400 direct jobs. At the time of the report, the investment had not yet been formally announced by Toyota.
