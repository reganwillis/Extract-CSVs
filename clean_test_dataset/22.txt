BMW to build electric vehicles in Mexico

Source URL: https://www.postguam.com/business/world/bmw-to-build-electric-vehicles-in-mexico/article_f532b628-a4fb-11ed-932d-038d6da82dca.html

FACTUAL PARAPHRASE

BMW announced an investment of about 800 million euros (approximately $866-$870 million at the time) at its San Luis Potosi plant in Mexico to prepare for electric-vehicle production.

The investment included production of high-voltage batteries and future fully electric vehicles based on BMW's Neue Klasse architecture. The project formed part of BMW's broader transition toward electric mobility and expansion of EV manufacturing capacity in North America.
