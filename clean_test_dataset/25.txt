Airbus first U.S. manufacturing facility in Mobile, Alabama

Source URL: https://www.areadevelopment.com/newsitems/7-3-2012/airbus-first-us-manufacturing-mobile-al-1251615.shtml

FACTUAL PARAPHRASE

Airbus announced in July 2012 that it would establish its first U.S.-based aircraft production facility at Brookley Aeroplex in Mobile, Alabama. The facility was planned to assemble and deliver A319, A320 and A321 aircraft.

Assembly was expected to begin in 2015, with first deliveries in 2016. Airbus projected that the Mobile line could produce roughly 40 to 50 aircraft per year by 2018. The project was part of Airbus' strategy to expand its global manufacturing footprint and better serve customers in the United States.
