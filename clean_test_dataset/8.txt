Nipro brings 232 new jobs and $397.8 million investment to Greenville-Pitt County

Source URL: https://www.investgreenvillenc.com/news-posts/nipro-brings-232-new-jobs-397-8m-investment-to-greenville-pitt-county-community

FACTUAL PARAPHRASE

Nipro Medical Corporation selected Greenville-Pitt County, North Carolina, for its first North American manufacturing center of excellence. The project involves $397.8 million in capital investment and 232 new jobs.

Nipro, owned by Japan-based Nipro Corporation, is a global healthcare and medical-device manufacturer. The company is purchasing a 130-acre, Pitt County-owned industrial site at 1911 Old Creek Road in Greenville. The announcement positioned the project as an expansion of eastern North Carolina's life-sciences and advanced-manufacturing sector.
