TMEIC manufacturing expansion in Waller County

Source URL: https://coveringkaty.com/news/waller/japanese-tech-giant-tmeic-invests-65-million-creating-green-/

FACTUAL PARAPHRASE

TMEIC Corporation Americas announced a new manufacturing facility in Waller County, Texas, for uninterruptible power supplies and medium-voltage drives. The project represents more than $65 million in capital investment and is expected to create 62 jobs initially.

Texas approved a Texas Enterprise Fund grant of $368,838 for the project. State officials described the investment as part of Texas' growth in advanced manufacturing and energy-related technology.
