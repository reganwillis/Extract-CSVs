IKO celebrates grand opening of new fiberglass and fiberglass-mat facilities in South Carolina

Source URL: https://www.iko.com/comm/press-release/iko-celebrates-grand-opening-of-new-fiberglass-and-fiberglass-mat-manufacturing-facilities-in-south-carolina/

FACTUAL PARAPHRASE

IKO North America celebrated the opening of new fiberglass and glass-mat manufacturing facilities in Chester County, South Carolina, in March 2026. The facilities represent more than $500 million in investment and create nearly 200 local jobs.

The project includes a fiberglass manufacturing plant and a 325,000-square-foot glass-mat plant near Chester. The glass-mat operation uses output from the adjacent fiberglass facility. Glass mat is an important input in asphalt-shingle manufacturing, and IKO said the vertically integrated facilities would improve supply-chain efficiency, quality control and reliability for its North American roofing-manufacturing network.

IKO is a family-owned roofing, waterproofing and insulation manufacturer founded in 1951 and operates more than 35 manufacturing plants in North America and Europe.
