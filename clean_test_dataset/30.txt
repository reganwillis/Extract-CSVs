Signature Foods USA selects Anderson County for first South Carolina operation

Source URL: https://governor.sc.gov/news/2026-04/signature-foods-usa-selects-anderson-county-first-south-carolina-operation

FACTUAL PARAPHRASE

Signature Foods USA selected Anderson County for its first South Carolina operation and first U.S. manufacturing facility. The company announced an $11.5 million investment that will create 202 jobs.

Signature Foods USA is part of Netherlands-based Signature Foods and produces refrigerated prepared foods such as meats and cheeses, dips, spreads, wraps and rolls. The new plant at 211 Pine Road in Easley is focused on producing dips and spreads for retail grocery deli departments. Operations were already underway when the project was announced in April 2026.
