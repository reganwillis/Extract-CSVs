LG Electronics to locate new U.S. manufacturing operations in Montgomery County

Source URL: https://tnecd.com/news/governor-haslam-tnecd-announce-lg-electronics-to-locate-new-u-s-manufacturing-operations-in-montgomery-county/

FACTUAL PARAPHRASE

LG Electronics announced plans for a new home-appliance manufacturing facility near Clarksville in Montgomery County, Tennessee. The project was initially described as an approximately 829,000-square-foot washing-machine plant representing $250 million in investment and at least 600 full-time jobs.

LG planned to manufacture front-load and top-load washing machines at the site, with the property providing room for possible future production of other home appliances. The company selected Tennessee after evaluating locations in multiple states.
