Maars North America selects Mount Pleasant for first U.S. production operation

Source URL: https://mountpleasantmade.com/maars-north-america-selects-mount-pleasant-for-first-u-s-production-operation/

FACTUAL PARAPHRASE

Maars North America selected Mount Pleasant, South Carolina, for its first U.S.-based production operation. The Netherlands-headquartered architectural-wall company announced a $3 million investment expected to create more than 10 jobs.

Maars produces customizable modular wall systems used in offices, hospitals, airports, universities and other commercial interiors and operates in more than 50 countries. Its facility at 437 Interlock Alley in Mount Pleasant will serve as a fabrication plant for the North American market and will include a showroom for the company's architectural products.
