Volkswagen announces second phase of Mexico investment of around $1 billion

Source URL: https://www.reuters.com/business/autos-transportation/volkswagen-announces-second-phase-mexico-investment-around-1-bln-2024-02-16/

FACTUAL PARAPHRASE

Volkswagen's Mexican unit announced an investment of roughly $1 billion in February 2024 as a second phase of spending intended to support its electric-vehicle business at existing operations in Puebla, Mexico.

The company did not specify which electric vehicles or components would be produced as a result of the investment. The new commitment followed a $763.5 million plan announced in 2022 for Volkswagen's Puebla complex, including a new paint plant and production line.

Volkswagen has produced models including the Jetta, Taos and Tiguan at Puebla. Audi, a Volkswagen Group company, has also manufactured the Q5 and a hybrid version at its Mexican operations.
