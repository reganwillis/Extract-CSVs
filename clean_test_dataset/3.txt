Nidec to expand manufacturing presence in Tennessee

Date: October 9, 2025
Source: Upper Cumberland Business Journal (UCBJ)
Source URL: https://www.ucbjournal.com/nidec-to-expand-manufacturing-presence-in-tennessee/

FACTUAL PARAPHRASE

Tennessee Gov. Bill Lee, Tennessee Department of Economic and Community Development Commissioner Stuart C. McWhorter, and Nidec officials announced that Nidec will expand its manufacturing operations in Henderson County, Tennessee.

Nidec Power, a division of Nidec Motor Corporation, manufactures advanced alternators for critical power-generation uses. Applications cited by the article include data centers, hospitals, and defense operations.

Nidec plans to invest $52.4 million in its existing facility in Lexington, Tennessee. The expansion is expected to create 200 new jobs. The project is intended to improve production efficiency and increase manufacturing capacity in response to growing electricity demand in the United States and international markets.

After the expansion is completed, Nidec Power is expected to employ more than 600 people in Henderson County.

Gov. Bill Lee said the investment would have a significant effect on the Henderson County community and emphasized the importance of job creation in rural Tennessee. He also credited Nidec's leadership and its long-term commitment to creating economic opportunities in the state.

Nidec was established in 1973. According to the article, it grew from an operation with four people into a global company with facilities on five continents. Its corporate headquarters are in Kyoto, Japan.

The article also describes Japan as Tennessee's leading foreign-direct-investment partner. More than 200 Japanese companies operate in Tennessee, collectively accounting for more than 57,000 jobs and approximately $20 billion in capital investment.
